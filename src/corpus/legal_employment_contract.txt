EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Artech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("Company") and the individual named in Schedule A ("Employee").

1. POSITION AND DUTIES
   1.1 The Employee is engaged in the role specified in Schedule A, reporting to the manager designated therein.
   1.2 The Employee agrees to devote their full professional time and attention to the Company's business and shall not engage in any competing activity without prior written consent.

2. COMPENSATION
   2.1 Base Salary: As specified in Schedule A, payable monthly on the last working day.
   2.2 Variable Pay: Performance-linked bonus up to 20% of annual base, subject to Company and individual performance metrics.
   2.3 Benefits: As per the Company's benefits policy in force from time to time (health insurance, PF, gratuity as mandated by law).

3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
   3.1 All work product, inventions, designs, software, or processes created by the Employee during the course of employment or using Company resources shall be the exclusive property of the Company.
   3.2 The Employee hereby assigns all rights, title, and interest in such work product to the Company.
   3.3 This clause survives termination of employment.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY
   4.1 The Employee agrees not to disclose, directly or indirectly, any Confidential Information of the Company to any third party during or after employment.
   4.2 "Confidential Information" includes but is not limited to: financial data, client lists, product roadmaps, pricing strategies, employee information, and proprietary processes.
   4.3 Breach of this clause entitles the Company to seek injunctive relief and damages.

5. NON-COMPETE AND NON-SOLICITATION
   5.1 For a period of 12 months post-termination, the Employee agrees not to directly or indirectly solicit any client or employee of the Company.
   5.2 The non-compete geographic scope: India and any country where the Company has active operations.

6. TERMINATION
   6.1 Notice Period: As per Schedule A (typically 60–90 days for permanent employees).
   6.2 The Company may terminate with immediate effect for cause (gross misconduct, fraud, policy violations).
   6.3 Garden Leave: During notice period, the Company may place the Employee on garden leave, maintaining salary but restricting access to systems.

7. GOVERNING LAW
   This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Disputes shall be subject to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, with seat in Mumbai.

Executed as of the date in Schedule A.
