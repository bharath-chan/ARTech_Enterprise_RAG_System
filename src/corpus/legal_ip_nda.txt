INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS AND NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT
Artech Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

This Agreement is entered into between Artech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("Disclosing Party / Company") and the Party identified in Appendix A ("Receiving Party").

ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by the Company, including but not limited to: software source code, algorithms, product designs, business strategies, financial projections, client identities, and employee compensation data.

1.2 "Intellectual Property" (IP) means inventions, discoveries, developments, improvements, software, works of authorship, databases, know-how, and trade secrets.

1.3 "Background IP" means IP owned by a party prior to the commencement of this Agreement.

ARTICLE 2: CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS

2.1 The Receiving Party shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent.

2.2 The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information only to employees or contractors who have a need to know and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less restrictive than this Agreement.

2.3 Permitted Disclosures: The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information as required by law or court order, provided they give the Company maximum notice reasonably possible and cooperate in seeking a protective order.

2.4 Duration: 
    - For employees: Perpetual for trade secrets; 3 years for other Confidential Information post-employment.
    - For partners/contractors: 3 years post-agreement termination.

ARTICLE 3: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT

3.1 All IP created by an employee or contractor during the course of their engagement with the Company, whether or not using Company resources, that relates to the Company's business, is and shall remain the exclusive property of the Company.

3.2 The Receiving Party hereby irrevocably assigns all rights, title, and interest in such IP to the Company, and agrees to execute any further instruments necessary to perfect such assignment.

3.3 Background IP retained by Receiving Party:
    - Pre-existing tools, libraries, or frameworks not developed for the Company remain the Receiving Party's property.
    - A schedule of claimed Background IP must be submitted at onboarding.

3.4 Open Source Restrictions:
    - No GPL-licensed or similarly viral open-source software may be incorporated into Company products without DPO and Engineering Director approval.
    - Use of MIT/Apache-licensed libraries is permitted with attribution compliance.

ARTICLE 4: REMEDIES

4.1 The parties acknowledge that breach of this Agreement would cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate. The Company shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedies available at law.

4.2 Liquidated damages for willful breach: ₹50 lakh or 3x the value of Confidential Information disclosed, whichever is greater.

ARTICLE 5: GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by Indian law. Disputes shall be resolved by arbitration in Mumbai under the UNCITRAL Rules.
